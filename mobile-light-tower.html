Portable Lighting Solutions

Mobile Light Towers for Demanding Worksites

Ariselux mobile light towers provide high-output LED illumination where fixed lighting is not practical. Our towable and portable lighting systems are designed for quick deployment, dependable night operation and demanding outdoor conditions.

Choose the Right Power System

Battery-operated light towers: quiet, zero-emission illumination for indoor, defence, maintenance and emergency applications.

Diesel mobile light towers: high-output, long-runtime lighting for construction, mining, highway and infrastructure work.

Solar and hybrid light towers: fuel-saving portable lighting for remote and environmentally sensitive sites.

Petrol and inflatable towers: compact solutions for events, rescue work, road projects and temporary lighting.
