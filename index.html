What's Unique About Our LED Technology?

We design & manufacture technologically advanced products that are versatile in function, simple to use, and effective for your progress.

Fuel Efficiency

Advanced combustion engineering and LED optimization reduce fuel consumption by up to 40%, delivering exceptional operating cost savings.

Longer Lifespan

Industrial LED lamps boast an ultra-long operating life exceeding 50,000 hours with negligible maintenance and zero downtime.

Incredible Light Distribution

Advanced directional optics in the LED light range provide exceptional light distribution with up to 5000 m² of illumination at 20 lux.

Heavy Duty Protection

Lamps are built with durable aluminum die-casting that ensures robust protection, even during transportation. High CRI (>85) for superior color accuracy.

Additional Standard Features Across All Towers

Integrated Lifting Point

Heavy-duty central crane lifting eye for rapid site loading and deployment.

Forklift Pockets

Reinforced chassis slots for effortless transport with standard forklifts and telehandlers.

Foldable Adjustable Towbar

Straight and foldable towing hitches compatible with standard commercial and industrial vehicles.
